## Learned User Preferences

- Prefer a Next.js marketing site with headless Shopify checkout, attributed as “Powered by Fail Up Inc.”
- Include PosePerfect Mat variants and bundle options on the site; do not offer the Wear+Pose or On-Set Starter bundles.
- Deploy and link the site on the owned domain poseperfectmats.com (Vercel).
- Prefer Loox video review widgets and Loox customer photos as additional social proof on landing pages.
- Brand as PosePerfect Mat™ (include the trademark mark); prefer sans-serif logo/wordmark text.
- Prefer a full add-to-cart experience with quantity controls, not checkout-only CTAs.
- Surface Instagram @poseperfectmats; include privacy policy, trademark information, SEO/AIO optimization, and the Shopify Meta Pixel.
- Prefer leaner landings: fewer sections, shorter section titles, and short persona nav labels (e.g. Corporate, Volume, Events).
- Avoid em dashes in on-page copy; use simpler punctuation instead.
- Prefer accurate product imagery per SKU, customer photos that show the mat in use, hero product visible above the fold on mobile, and slower image carousels.
